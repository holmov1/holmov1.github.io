About

Hey, I'm Ali, a CS master's student at TUM. Currently, I'm interested in mechanistic interpretability, and the question that excites me most is how deep learning models represent knowledge. I also like building things. Right now I'm working part-time at Helmit on ML models for online child safety.

Before that, I did research projects at EML Munich (user models, belief extraction), hessian.AI (knowledge editing) and King's College London (AI safety), and studied CS at Marburg & philosophy at Saint Petersburg State University. When I'm not working, I like to swim and fence, read (mostly fiction & poetry), and do jigsaw puzzles or sudoku. I also have a thing for medieval bestiary art, which explains the creatures on this site.

P.S. I dropped some letters from my surname (Kholmovaia) in 2026 and now use the simplified version.
